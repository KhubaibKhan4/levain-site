Levain: Sourdough Starter

Feeding tracker, peak reminders, bread calculator and bake planner for iPhone and Android.

Levain reminds you when to feed your starter, estimates when it will peak, does the bread math and plans bake day backwards from when you want to eat. There's no account, and your starters, feedings and photos stay on your phone. The free version shows ads; Levain Pro removes them.

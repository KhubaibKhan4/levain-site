Privacy Policy

Levain: Sourdough Starter · Effective 29 September 2026

The short version. Levain works without an account, and what you enter stays on your phone. We don't run a server that receives your data. The free version shows ads from Google and its partners, which use device and usage information to show and measure ads; you choose whether that can be used to track you, and Levain Pro removes ads entirely. Apart from ads, the only data that leaves your phone is what's needed to confirm purchases and to report crashes.

This policy covers the Levain: Sourdough Starter app for iOS and Android (“Levain”, “the app”), made by Muhammad Khubaib Imtiaz (“we”, “us”).

What stays on your phone

  • Your starters, feedings, peak notes, formulas, bake plans and settings, kept in the app's private storage.
  • Photos you choose to attach. The app saves a smaller copy and removes any location data the original photo carries. The app only sees the photos you pick, and never uploads them.
  • Reminders, which your phone schedules as local notifications. Nothing is sent over the network to deliver them.

We have no copy of any of this. If your phone backs up apps to your iCloud or Google account, Levain's data may be included in that backup, which is stored in your account under Apple's or Google's terms. We can't access it. Deleting the app deletes the data on your phone.

Data handled by other services

Purchases

Levain Pro is bought through the App Store or Google Play. Apple and Google process the payment under their own privacy policies; we never see your name or payment details. We use RevenueCat to confirm which purchases are active. RevenueCat receives an anonymous ID created by the app, your purchase history for Levain, and technical details any network request carries, such as device type, operating system, country and IP address. This isn't linked to your identity and is used only to unlock Pro and restore purchases.

Crash reports

If the app crashes or hits a serious error, a report is sent to Sentry so we can fix it. A report contains the device model, operating system and app version, and a technical record of what the app was doing when the error happened. It doesn't include your starters, notes, photos or anything that identifies you, and it is deleted automatically after at most 90 days.

Ads (free version only)

The free version shows ads through Google AdMob, which also requests ads from Unity Ads, AppLovin and Liftoff. To choose, show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, these services receive:

  • your device's advertising identifier (the IDFA on iPhone or the advertising ID on Android), where you allow it;
  • your IP address, which gives a rough idea of where you are (not your precise location);
  • how you interact with ads and the app, such as app launches, ad views and taps;
  • technical details such as device model, operating system and app performance.

They don't receive your starters, notes or photos. Each network uses this under its own privacy policy, linked above. Watching an optional ad on the Levain Pro screen unlocks Pro for 24 hours; that works the same way.

What we don't do

  • No accounts, sign-in or profiles.
  • No ads for Levain Pro subscribers.
  • No selling of your data, and no sharing with data brokers.
  • No precise location, contacts or microphone access.

Exports you make

“Export feedings as CSV” creates a file of your feeding log and opens your phone's share sheet. Where it goes next is your choice; we never receive it.

Your choices and rights

You can see, change and delete everything you entered from within the app, and deleting the app removes it all from your phone.

  • Tracking on iPhone. Levain asks before your activity can be used to track you across other companies' apps and websites. You can change this any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. If you say no, you still see ads, but they aren't personalized.
  • Ad consent. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Levain asks for your consent before showing personalized ads. You can change your choice in Levain's Settings under “Ad privacy choices”.
  • Advertising ID on Android. You can reset or delete it in your phone's settings under Privacy → Ads.
  • No ads at all. Levain Pro removes all ads.

Because purchase, crash and ad data aren't linked to your name or email, we usually can't find records that belong to you, but if you'd like us to try to access or delete them, email us and we'll help. You may also have rights under laws such as the GDPR or CCPA; contact us to use them.

Children

Levain is for a general audience and isn't directed at children. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, and the app's ads aren't meant for them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll post the new version on this page with a new effective date.

Common questions

My feeding reminders don't appear

  • Check that notifications are allowed for Levain in your phone's Settings.
  • On iPhone, a Focus mode can hide them. Allow Levain in the Focus, or turn Focus off to test.
  • On Android, some phones stop apps in the background to save battery. Set Levain's battery usage to “Unrestricted”, and allow “Alarms & reminders” if your phone asks.

The peak estimate is off for my starter

The first estimates are based on the flour, feeding ratio and room temperature. Each time you mark when your starter actually peaked, Levain learns how your starter behaves and the estimates get closer.

How do I restore Levain Pro on a new phone?

Sign in with the same Apple ID or Google account you bought it with, open Levain's Settings and tap Restore purchases.

Why does Levain show ads, and how do I remove them?

Ads keep the free version free. You'll see a small banner in the calculators and, now and then, a full-screen ad when you leave them; never during a feeding or a reminder. Levain Pro removes every ad. You can also watch an optional ad on the Levain Pro screen for 24 hours of Pro.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

  • iPhone: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking controls whether ads can be personalized using your activity in other apps.
  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland: open Levain's Settings and tap “Ad privacy choices”.
  • Android: your phone's Settings → Privacy → Ads lets you reset or delete your advertising ID.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple or Google, not in the app. Deleting the app doesn't cancel them.

  • iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions, or manage subscriptions.
  • Android: Play Store → your profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions, or manage subscriptions.

If you cancel during the free trial, you won't be charged. The lifetime purchase is a one-time payment and never renews.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by the store you paid through: Apple or Google Play.

Will my starters move to a new phone?

Levain has no account or cloud sync, so your data lives on your phone. It's usually carried over when you move to a new phone using your phone's own backup or transfer (iCloud backup or Quick Start on iPhone, Google backup on Android). To keep your own copy of the feeding log, use Export feedings as CSV in Settings (Levain Pro).

What happens to my data if I delete the app?

It's deleted from your phone, and we have no copy. See the privacy policy for details.
